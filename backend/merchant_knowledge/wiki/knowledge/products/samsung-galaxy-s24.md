## Samsung Galaxy S24 Overview

The Samsung Galaxy S24 is a premium smartphone priced at **₹74,999**, featuring a 6.2‑inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display and a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor paired with 8 GB RAM.
